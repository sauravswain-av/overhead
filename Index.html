The context

The project it was built for

Overhead is the interface for an outbound telesales console — agents dial businesses from a list all day and the screen has to tell them who to call, why, and what to do next. That single job, repeated 78 times a day, is the constraint the whole system is designed against.

Motion

Everything below is running the real animation on a loop. Motion here has one job: make the card swap feel instant so the agent never waits between dials.

Duration — three tokens travelling the same distance
120ms
micro
Hover, focus ring, checkbox, disposition selection. Fast enough to feel caused by the key press.
180ms
standard
Card swap, panel disclosure, toast, tooltip. The default — 20ms faster than a typical design system because it fires 78 times a day.
260ms
large
Wrap panel rising, drawer, modal. Only for surfaces that cover more than half the centre column.
There is no fourth duration and no entrance animation on the console. The next-call card cross-fades in 180ms; it does not slide, scale or stagger. A staggered entrance on a card the agent sees 78 times reads as a stutter by the tenth repetition.
Easing — one curve
cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1)

Leaves fast, arrives slow, no overshoot. The dashed diagonal is linear for comparison.

No spring, no bounce. A bouncing card in a calling tool reads as playful at 09:30 and as mockery at 17:45.

Dialer & telephony

Overhead is a click-to-call console, not a predictive dialer. That is a product decision with a design consequence on every screen.

Why not predictive
✕ Predictive
System dials 3 numbers, connects the first answer to whichever agent is free. The agent hears a voice before they see a name. Abandoned calls when nobody is free. Regulated or banned in several markets.
✓ Click-to-call
The agent sees the card, presses space, and the line dials. They know who they are calling before it rings. No abandoned calls. The whole "who to call next" problem is solved by the queue instead of by the dialer.
The design consequence. Because the agent chooses the moment, the 1.5 seconds between the card appearing and the dial are real and usable — which is why the card carries exactly three facts and one reason. In a predictive dialer that window does not exist and the card would be pointless.

Versioning

Libraries publish on a two-week cycle. Breaking changes to the call bar or the next-call card require the attention-budget audit in §68 to be re-run and re-signed.

What is deliberately missing

No tablet frames, no agent mobile, no wallboard, no dark theme. Each was considered and each would have added surface without answering who, order or context.
